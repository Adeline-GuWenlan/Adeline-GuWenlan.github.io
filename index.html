Research Experience

Spring 2026 – Present

Optimizer Geometry in Equivariant Force Fields

Undergraduate Researcher · Prof. Shengjie Wang Lab, NYU Shanghai

  • Built MACEBench to study how AdamW, Muon and SOAP shape equivariant force fields; implemented e3nn matrix routing and tensor-product path-aware blocking.
  • Implemented force-loss Hessian-vector products, Lanczos/Hutchinson curvature estimates and block-normalized 2D loss surfaces; built linear mode-connectivity probes with permutation alignment.
  • Analyzed weight/update spectra, stable/effective ranks and singular subspaces to isolate optimizer effects.
May 2026 – Present

Neural Solvers for Mean-Field Games

DURF Research Fellow · Prof. Mathieu Laurière Lab, NYU Shanghai

  • Built Riccati/fixed-point and Deep BSDE solvers for stochastic flocking equilibria; used the closed-form linear-quadratic case to validate learned adjoints.
  • Studied sensitivity to training and finite-agent sampling, and the limits of convergence in nonlinear regimes.
Jan. – May 2026

Learned Initialization for DFT

Undergraduate Researcher · Prof. Shengjie Wang Lab, NYU Shanghai

  • Ran ablations across 13 molecules and built a provenance-tracked checkpoint harness (60-row benchmark table).
