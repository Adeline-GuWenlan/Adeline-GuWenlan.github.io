About

I'm a Data Science (AI concentration) student with a minor in Mathematics at NYU Shanghai, studying away at NYU New York for Fall 2026 – Spring 2027. My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and the physical sciences: how optimizers shape equivariant force fields, neural solvers for mean-field games, and learned initialization for DFT.

I am grateful to be supported by the NYU Shanghai Recognition Award and the Dean's Undergraduate Research Fund for two consecutive years.
